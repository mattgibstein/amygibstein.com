About Amy

For more than three decades, Amy Gibstein has helped people find art they love – and create homes worthy of it.

She began her career as an education curator at the Whitney Museum in New York. At ArtSmart, she built the first art collections for some of the world’s largest corporations.

After moving to Miami, where she raised her two sons, Amy turned to private clients. Today she advises collectors on the art they acquire and designs the homes that art lives in, from a single room to an entire residence.

“Live with art you love, and your home will tell your story.”

– Amy Gibstein
